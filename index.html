Why it's hard

Growing at big-box home improvement is its own game.

01

The line review is won before the meeting.

Most brands walk in with a deck. The merchant is looking for a plan they can say yes to, backed by sell-through, margin, and a real reason to give you the space.

02

The scorecard is unforgiving.

Sell-through, in-stock, and vendor funding decide whether you keep the space you fought for. Miss on them and the next reset takes it right back.

03

Specialty and DTC playbooks don't travel.

What works on your own site or in a specialty store doesn't automatically fit a Home Depot or Lowe's planogram. The channel runs on its own rules, and they don't bend for you.
